# Brand Identity — Kiseh Hamelech Designs

## The name is the strategy

**כס המלך** — *Kiseh HaMelech* — means **"the King's Throne."** For a chair-maker this is a gift: the product category (a chair) and the brand promise (royalty, honor, elevation) are the same word. Everything we say should quietly cash in on this.

The name also resonates deeply with the target audience's world:
- **Shabbat is called *Shabbat HaMalka*** — "the Sabbath Queen." A family that honors Shabbat is, in a real sense, seating royalty at its table every week.
- **"כבוד שבת" / *Kavod Shabbat*** (honoring the Sabbath) is a lived value. Beautiful furniture for the Shabbat table is not vanity — it is *hiddur*, the beautification of a mitzvah.
- A well-set table where generations gather is a small throne room.

**Tagline directions (Hebrew):**
- `כל כיסא — כמו כס מלכות.` ("Every chair — like a royal throne.")
- `הכבוד שמגיע לשולחן שלכם.` ("The honor your table deserves.")
- `רהיטים שנשארים במשפחה. לדורות.` ("Furniture that stays in the family. For generations.")

## Positioning

> Kas Hamelech makes **bespoke, handmade upholstered furniture** for families who want their home — and above all their Shabbat and Chag table — to feel dignified, warm, and built to last for generations.

We compete on **craftsmanship + customization + longevity**, not on price. We are the opposite of flat-pack, mass-produced, throwaway furniture. The right customer is not looking for the cheapest chair; they are looking for the *right* chair, made for their home, that will survive decades of large family meals.

**We are:** artisanal, warm, rooted, reverent, uncompromising on quality.
**We are not:** trendy-disposable, mass-market, impersonal, discount-driven.

## Core value proposition

1. **Bespoke / made-to-order (בהתאמה אישית)** — every piece is built to the customer's space, fabric, color, and needs.
2. **Genuine craftsmanship (עבודת יד)** — handmade, reinforced frames, quality upholstery that endures.
3. **Built for real family life** — durable enough for large families, frequent guests (*hachnasat orchim*), and weekly Shabbat use.
4. **Made in Israel, personal service** — direct relationship with the maker; studio visits; fast, human WhatsApp response.
5. **An heirloom, not a purchase** — furniture that lasts and can be reupholstered rather than replaced.

## Brand voice

- **Warm and respectful**, the way you'd speak to a valued guest in your home.
- **Confident about quality**, never boastful or salesy. Let the craftsmanship talk.
- **Culturally fluent and reverent** — uses religious/Shabbat references naturally and correctly, never as a gimmick.
- **Concise.** Hebrew-first. Short lines, clear next step (usually: message us on WhatsApp).
- Tasteful emoji use: 👑 🪑 🛋️ ✨ — sparingly, as accents.

**Voice do / don't**
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "כיסא שנבנה בדיוק בשביל השולחן שלכם." | "המבצע הכי זול בשוק!" |
| "עבודת יד, מסגרת מחוזקת, בד לבחירתכם." | Generic "best quality, great prices" |
| Reference Shabbat/Chag hosting sincerely | Use religious themes as a punchline |
| One clear call to action | Five links and a wall of text |

## Visual identity (guidance for creative)

- **Palette:** warm, regal, natural — deep wood tones, cream/ivory upholstery, muted royal accents (burgundy, deep green, navy, gold as a *restrained* accent). Avoid neon, avoid cold minimalism.
- **Photography:** the product *in a real, set table / warm home*, ideally styled for Shabbat or Chag (challah, candles, set table) — but tasteful, never cluttered. Close-ups that show stitching, frame, fabric texture = "proof of craft."
- **Motion (TikTok/Reels):** the *making* of a chair (frame → upholstery → finished piece), the fabric-selection moment, before/after reupholstery. Process is our best content.
- **Logo mark:** the 👑 crown motif pairs naturally with the name; keep typography classic/serif, not startup-modern.
