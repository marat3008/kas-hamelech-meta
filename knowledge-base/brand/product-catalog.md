## Craftsmanship proof points (use these as ad substance, not adjectives)

Marketing that only says "high quality" is weak. Marketing that shows *why* it's high quality converts. Use concrete, verifiable proof points:

- **Handmade upholstery (עבודת יד)** — not machine-assembled flat-pack.
- **Reinforced frame** built to survive daily use by a large family and frequent guests.
- **Customer-chosen fabric & color (בד וצבע לבחירתכם)** — matched to the room.
- **Made to the exact dimensions** of the customer's space / table.
- **Reupholstery capability** — the piece can be renewed in years to come rather than thrown away (heirloom logic).
- **Direct-from-the-maker** — no middleman; the person who builds it is the person you talk to.

## How products map to the audience's needs

- **Big Shabbat/Chag meals, many guests** → need chairs that are *comfortable, dignified, and durable*, in quantity, matched as a set. → Dining chairs (flagship).
- **Hosting / receiving guests (hachnasat orchim)** → the living room and its seating are a statement of *kavod*. → Armchairs & sofas.
- **Existing beloved furniture that's worn** → don't replace, restore. → Reupholstery.
- **Furnishing for a new home / newly married couple** → building a home meant to last. → Full sets, consultative sale.
