# Channel Playbooks — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok

Each channel plays a different role. Don't cross-post blindly; adapt format, tone, and segment.

---

## Instagram — `@kas_hamelech` (primary brand home)

**Role:** the brand's showroom and proof engine. Best fit for the Dati Leumi / design-aware segment.

**Formats & what wins**
- **Reels (priority):** "the making of" a chair, fabric-selection moments, before/after reupholstery, styled Shabbat/Chag table reveals. 7–20s, hook in first 1s, captions on-screen (many watch muted).
- **Carousels:** before/after (slide 1 = after, or swipe reveal), fabric/color range, "5 questions to ask before ordering custom chairs," customer stories.
- **Feed posts:** hero product shots in a warm home; craftsmanship close-ups (stitching, frame).
- **Stories:** behind-the-scenes daily, polls ("which fabric?"), studio moments, reshared customer photos, link/CTA stickers to WhatsApp.
- **Highlights:** curate — Before/After · Process · Shabbat Table · Reviews · Studio Visit.

**Paid:** Click-to-WhatsApp and Lead Ads from the best-performing organic Reels (whitelisted/boosted). Retarget video viewers and profile engagers.

**Cadence:** 3–5 posts/week + daily Stories, **none during Shabbat/Chag**. Pre-schedule so nothing publishes on Shabbat.

---

## Facebook (reach, older & broader, community)

**Role:** widest reach across segments, strong for **Lead Ads**, community groups, and an older demographic that still buys big-ticket furniture. Often the best CPL for this audience.

**Formats & what wins**
- **Lead Ads** (in-platform form → WhatsApp follow-up via `meta-system`). Lowest friction; great for "book a studio visit" / "get a quote."
- **Click-to-WhatsApp** — same spine as IG.
- **Carousels & single-image** with before/after and testimonials.
- **Facebook Page** posts styled for the family/Shabbat angle; Reels cross-posted from IG.

**Paid:** this is usually where the efficient conversion budget sits for this audience. Use CAPI (`capi/conversions.js`) so `Lead`/`Contact` optimize properly.

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts/week, Lead Ads always-on (paused for Shabbat/Chag), heavier before Chagim.

---

## TikTok (top-of-funnel discovery & craft storytelling)

**Role:** cheap reach and *craft fascination*. Process content performs disproportionately well here. Best for the younger Dati Leumi segment and brand awareness. **Least suited to the Haredi segment** — weight accordingly.

**Formats & what wins**
- **Process / satisfying content:** raw frame → padding → fabric → finished chair; the "reveal." Oddly-satisfying upholstery clips travel far.
- **Before/After reupholstery** — a native TikTok winner.
- **"POV: setting the Shabbat table"** style storytelling, tasteful and warm.
- **Trends adapted tastefully** to the brand — but never at the cost of dignity or tzniut.
- Native, vertical, hook in first 1s, on-screen text, trending-but-tasteful audio.

**Paid:** TikTok Spark Ads on the best organic clips → drive to profile / link / WhatsApp. Keep TikTok mostly awareness; convert on Meta/WhatsApp.

**Cadence:** 3–7 short videos/week (volume matters on TikTok), **none during Shabbat/Chag.**

---

## Cross-channel content engine (make once, cut many)

One studio shoot should yield weeks of content:
1. Film a full build + a fabric-selection session + a before/after in one session.
2. Cut vertical Reels/TikToks (process, reveal, before/after).
3. Pull stills for IG feed + FB + carousels.
4. Write captions per channel from `creative/ad-copy-library.md`.
5. Tag the winners; promote only what earns it organically.
